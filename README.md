#### About Dataset
A comprehensive dataset covering the global impact of AI agents on jobs, skills, salaries, and industries across 12 countries and 12 industry sectors. The dataset spans from January 2022 to March 2026 and represents the full landscape of AI adoption — from automation risk and workforce displacement to the emergence of new AI-driven roles, productivity gains, and evolving skill demands across all experience levels.
#### Goal
To build a Power BI dashboard on AI Agents & the Future of Work that identifies which jobs are at risk of automation, tracks which roles are booming due to AI adoption.
#### What the Dashboard helps with
* Automation risk distribution across job roles and job categories, and experience levels from Entry Level to Executive/Lead.
* AI adoption rates, investment levels ($B), and jobs at risk vs jobs created across industries.
* Salary trends, salary change across countries.
* AI tool performance comparison across tools using Capability, Safety, Reasoning, Coding, and Creativity scores.
* Productivity gain, cost reduction, and time saved per week from AI adoption, alongside AI investment and job security scoring across fact.
#### Tool Used
Power Bi
#### Key Insights
* AI is creating more jobs (127M) than it destroys (111M) — but the new jobs need different skills.
* Data Entry and Telemarketing jobs are most at risk — over 90% chance of being replaced by AI.
* UAE is adopting AI fastest (61%) — even faster than the USA (54%) which spends the most on AI.
* Tech is the biggest winner — uses AI the most and still creating 22 million new jobs.
* Jobs like AI Governance Analyst, NLP and ML engineer have the least chance of being replaced by AI. 
* Retail workers are most at risk — 18 million jobs could disappear, mostly cashiers and warehouse roles. 
* Healthcare is growing — AI is helping create 18 million new healthcare jobs. 
* Government is the slowest — only 35% of public sector uses AI, far behind everyone else. 
* USA pays the most ($161K) and India pays the least ($17K) — AI benefits are not reaching everyone equally.
* ChatGPT is the most popular — 500 million people use it every month, more than all other tools combined. 
* Gemini is second — 300 million users, Google's AI is growing fast. 
* Claude is the least used but highest quality — only 100M users but scores best in safety and reasoning. 
* Education spends the most on AI — surprisingly the biggest AI investor despite having fewer jobs at risk.
